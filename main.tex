Given \(a=x_0<\cdots<x_n=b\) and \(\xi_i\in[x_{i-1},x_i]\), the Riemann
integral of a bounded function \(f\) on \([a,b]\) is the tag-independent
limit, when it exists,

\[
  \int_a^b f(x)\,dx
  := \lim_{\max_i (x_i-x_{i-1}) \to 0}
  \sum_{i=1}^n f(\xi_i)(x_i-x_{i-1}).
\]
